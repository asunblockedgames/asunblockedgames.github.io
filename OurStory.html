Our Story

Founded by CupSponge_1234, Saman Unblocked Games was the first version of the site on September 26, 2024 and released on the 27th. A issue with Google had the site migrated to GitHub in November launching December 9th, 2024. A site name change was done to AS Unblocked Games (current) on January 6th, 2025. On March 1st, we lost our website due to copyright strikes but relaunched on March 18th.
