Our Story

Founded by CupSponge_1234, Saman Unblocked Games was the first version of the site on September 26, 2024 and released on the 27th. A issue with Google had the site migrated to GitHub in November launching December 9th, 2024. A site name change was done to AS Unblocked Games (current) on January 6th, 2025. March 1st, we lost our website due to copyright strikes but relaunched on March 18th. BREAKING NEW: As of July 25th, 2026, All current AS Unblocked Games developers have been laid off, and have been replaced by our new developer, developeravava.
